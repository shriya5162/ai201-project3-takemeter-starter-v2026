# the definitions I wrote. baseline.py reads this file.

critique: Makes a claim about a book, author, or reading and backs it with at least one specific reason: a named feature of the text (a character, scene, style, or structure), a fact, or a firsthand example.
hot_take: States a confident verdict or generalization about a book, author, genre, or readers, with no specific reason offered.
reaction: Describes the writer's own feelings, experience, or reading habits, without judging the quality of a work or author and without making a claim about the world that someone else could argue with.
